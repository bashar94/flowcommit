# Code of conduct

FlowCommit is built by and for people with every level of experience, including people who
are writing their first app with AI. We want everyone to feel welcome asking questions.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, especially with beginners.
- Assume good intent, and give feedback about the work, not the person.
- Accept that maintainers sometimes say no, and explain why when they do.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language
- Personal attacks, trolling or deliberately derailing discussions
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwelcome attention

## If something happens

Contact the maintainers privately through GitHub. Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers
may remove comments, code or issues, or block people who don't follow this code, in any
space the project runs: the repository, issues, discussions and community chats.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
