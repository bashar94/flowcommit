# The FlowCommit name and logo

The FlowCommit code is open source under the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE). The name
"FlowCommit" and the FlowCommit logo are not part of that license (see section 6 of it):
they belong to the project's creator, so that people can trust that something called
FlowCommit really is FlowCommit.

## You're welcome to

- Say your project works with, integrates with, or is built on FlowCommit
- Write about FlowCommit, make tutorials and videos, and use the name and logo to refer to it
- Use the name and logo unchanged in community events, talks and meetups about FlowCommit
- Build and share plugins and templates for FlowCommit, named like "Kanban templates for
  FlowCommit"

## Please ask first before you

- Release a changed copy (a fork) under the FlowCommit name. Forks are welcome, but under a
  different name, with a note that it's based on FlowCommit.
- Offer a hosted or paid service called FlowCommit, or with FlowCommit in its name
- Use the name or logo in a way that suggests the project endorses you, or in your own
  product's logo, domain or app name

Questions, or want permission? Open an issue or contact the maintainers through GitHub.
